opengrid — calculated measures

Rows: country · columns: year · the quantity, and the same quantity as each country's share of its year. A measure with calc is shown as a share of its row, its column or the grand total, or as a running total along the columns — computed by the source, here the engine in a worker, by the rules a server follows too.

In the toolbar, the button beside a measure's name says what the measure is shown as and changes it. A share is a fraction between 0 and 1; this page formats it as a percentage, for every measure that is shown as one.

Serve the page with just serve-demo. The pivot without calculations is browser.html.
